## Platform

## Users

1. **Citizens / Field Healthcare & Community Workers**: Submit on-the-ground challenges (e.g. rural triage delays, water contamination, civic infrastructure failures) with location, severity, and photo/video evidence.
2. **Universities / Faculty Mentors / Student Innovators**: Accept structured challenges, lead capability-matched R&D, form interdisciplinary teams, and develop prototypes for real-world deployment.
3. **Industry / Startups / CSR Partners**: Provide Schedule VII funding, technical mentorship, production-grade hardware/software, and testing resources for field pilots.
4. **Government / Nodal Officers / Domain Validators**: Oversee problem prioritization, approve pilot clearances, and conduct independent verification audits.

## Product Purpose

SETU is a closed-loop innovation and execution platform that converts validated societal challenges into accountable university-led projects, enables industry/CSR co-sponsorship, and verifies measurable, real-world outcomes.

Success is measured exclusively by **Verified Societal Outcomes (VSOs)**—proving that a reported community problem follows a traceable path to tested, verified, and permanent societal improvement.

## Positioning

**"No VSO without evidence."**

Unlike grievance portals that terminate at administrative complaint filing, and unlike student hackathon showcases that terminate at prototype presentations, SETU establishes persistent, accountable lifecycle governance:
```
Citizen Problem → SETU AI Structuring → University R&D → Industry/CSR Support → Real-World Pilot → Verified Societal Outcome (VSO) → Controlled Reuse
```

## Operating Context

- **Flagship Demo Flow (Healthcare)**:
  1. A rural community healthcare worker reports severe triage bottlenecks and prolonged patient wait times at a Primary Health Centre (PHC).
  2. SETU structures, validates, and prioritizes the problem.
  3. A university engineering/health informatics team accepts the challenge and proposes an edge-deployed queue-management and priority triage solution.
  4. A healthtech startup/CSR partner provides IoT hardware, telemetry, and pilot funding.
  5. The solution is deployed in an active rural PHC setting.
  6. Patient waiting times and triage delays are measured against pre-established baselines.
  7. Independent multi-party audit certifies the outcome, publishing an official VSO.

- **SETU-VSO 5-Stage Verification Framework**:
  1. *Problem Verified*: Genuine societal need confirmed by a credible local stakeholder.
  2. *Solution Implemented*: Operational solution deployed in a real-world setting (not a lab mockup).
  3. *Outcome Measured*: Specific KPIs defined prior to pilot and measured against baseline.
  4. *Impact Validated*: Independent review by university faculty, industry partner, and nodal official.
  5. *Evidence Published*: Publicly auditable verification record with methodology, raw data, and authority sign-offs.

- **VSO Tier Badging**:
  - 🟡 **Pilot Verified**: Solution actively deployed and operating in a real environment.
  - 🟢 **Outcome Verified**: Statistically significant, measurable improvement demonstrated against baseline.
  - 🔵 **Impact Verified**: Sustained, independently audited societal outcome maintained over time.

## Capabilities and Constraints

### Core Modules (MVP Scope)
1. **Problem Discovery**: Guided wizard for citizens and field workers to capture location, domain, urgency, affected population, and multimedia evidence.
2. **Problem Structuring & Prioritization**: AI-assisted structuring, semantic deduplication, and severity ranking with mandatory human-in-the-loop review.
3. **Solution Ecosystem**: Capability-based matching connecting validated challenges to equipped university labs, researchers, and startups.
4. **Pilot Execution**: Milestone tracking, grant disbursement milestones, stakeholder coordination, and deployment logging.
5. **Verified Societal Outcomes**: KPI verification engine comparing pre-pilot vs post-pilot telemetry and hosting audit records.
6. **Executive Impact Dashboard**: Transparent visual pipeline showing problems progressing from intake to verified impact.

### Scope Boundaries
- Not an open, uncurated social network or discussion forum.
- Not a generic freelance or project bidding marketplace.
- Not a black-box autonomous AI decision engine; all impactful decisions remain human-governed.

## Product Principles

1. **Outcome Over Volume**: Never optimize for submission count or superficial user signups. The only valid metric of success is verified real-world improvement.
2. **No VSO Without Evidence**: Every outcome claim requires pre-pilot baseline metrics, real-world deployment telemetry, and independent multi-stakeholder certification.
3. **Human-Governed Intelligence**: AI structures and matches; authorized human domain experts and nodal officers decide.
4. **Accountability by Lifecycle**: Every challenge has a transparent owner, verified state, next action, and timeline SLA.
5. **Solution Reuse**: Verified solutions become open architectural models to accelerate resolution of recurring challenges nationwide.
